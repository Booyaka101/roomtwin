RoomTwin

This is the real RoomTwin Home Assistant card, running on a scan of a real room. The home behind it is simulated in your browser, so nothing here controls actual devices.

Tap a light to switch it and watch its part of the room change. Press and hold a pin for its details and colour controls. Drag to look around, pinch or hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom. The pencil opens the editor you'd use to place pins in your own room. Changes aren't saved anywhere.

Want it for your own rooms? The README covers scanning a room and installing the card with HACS.
