Your room, in 3D, on your dashboard.

A Home Assistant card that shows a scan of your actual room with every light and sensor pinned where it really is. Tap the lamp and the lamp turns on, and that part of the room brightens with it.

This is the real card running on a scan of a real playroom. The home behind it is simulated in your browser, so nothing here controls actual devices and nothing you change is saved.

  • Tap a light to switch it
  • Hold a pin for its details
  • Drag to look around
  • Pinch or Ctrl and scroll to zoom
  • The pencil opens the editor
